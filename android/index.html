Get the app

Android beta.

Rimshot for Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Follow these three steps on your Android phone and you're in.

  1. Join the Google Group

    Google Play lets in members of this group. Join with the Google account you use on your phone.

    Join the group

  2. Join the testing program

    Open the testing page and tap Become a tester.

    Become a tester

  3. Download the app

    Give it a few minutes first. Google Play takes a little while to recognize you as a tester. Until then, it might say the app isn't available.

    Get it on Google Play

Sorry for the extra hoops. Google Play requires a closed test before Rimshot can launch for everyone. Thanks for helping out! — Neil

Trouble joining?

Google Play says the app isn't available.

Check that you joined the group and became a tester with the same Google account that's signed in to the Play Store on your phone. After you join, it can take a few minutes for access to go through.

The testing page won't load.

Join the Google Group first. The testing page only opens for group members. If you have more than one Google account, switch to the one you joined with.
