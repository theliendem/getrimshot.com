Questions.

Something else? Email support@getrimshot.com.

Is Rimshot free?

Yes. Rimshot is free to download and has no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Is Rimshot on Android?

Yes, as a closed beta on Google Play. It takes three quick steps to join. See how to get it.

Can a pit with iPhones and Android phones share a show?

Yes. Rimshot is on the App Store and in closed beta on Android, and both can send and open shows. A shared link also plays in any phone's browser, so whoever hasn't installed Rimshot yet can still hear it.

Is Rimshot only for drummers?

No. The name comes from drumming, but Rimshot is a metronome for anyone whose music changes tempo or time signature: pit players, singers, band sections and soloists.

How do I practice just a tempo change?

Build the piece as a show, then set the loop to start a couple of bars before the change and end a couple of bars after it. Rimshot plays those bars, counts off two measures, and goes again until you stop it.

What's a show?

A show is a sequence of measure groups. Each group sets how many measures to play, the tempo and the time signature. Press play and Rimshot runs the whole thing in order, so you can rehearse a piece from start to finish without touching your phone.

Can it speed up or slow down gradually?

Yes. Turn a group into a tempo ramp and Rimshot moves from one tempo to the next beat by beat, for an accelerando or a ritardando. A ramp can start from wherever the group before it left off.

Can I loop just one section?

Yes. Pick where to start and stop, from the beginning, any measure or any section letter, and Rimshot repeats it with a two-measure count-off between passes. You can also add a two-measure count-in before the show starts.

Can I share a show with my band?

Yes. Share a show as a link or QR code. Anyone who opens it can hear it play in their browser, then open it in Rimshot to practice.

Will it keep playing when my screen is locked?

Yes. Turn on Background Playback in Settings and Rimshot keeps time while you switch apps or lock your phone.

Does the tuner record me?

Never. The tuner listens through the microphone and works out the pitch in real time, on your phone. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Can I tune to something other than A = 440?

Yes. Change the reference pitch in Settings, and note names and cents follow it.

Does Rimshot need an internet connection?

Nope! The metronome, shows and tuner all run on your device.
